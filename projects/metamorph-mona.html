Metamorph M.O.N.A.

A generative architectural composition built from the four letters of a name, where parametric geometry becomes spatial identity, and form refuses to stand still.

Workshop DigitalFutures 2022
Tools Grasshopper · Rhino · Twinmotion
Type Generative · Material · Atmosphere
01 · Concept

Letter-Based Morphology &
Parametric Identity

Metamorph M.O.N.A. is a generative architectural composition built from the four letters of my name (M, O, N, A) parametrically modeled in Grasshopper and brought to life through Rhino and Twinmotion. What began as a formal exercise in letter-based morphology evolved into a spatial investigation: how can the geometry of language become architecture? How can a name become a place?

The oblique, fragmented forms carry a deliberate sense of dynamism and unresolved tension: they do not settle. Sharp angles interrupt smooth surfaces; light fractures across metallic planes; reflections dissolve into water. The composition refuses stillness, insisting instead on transformation as its primary condition. This is not a static object. It is a space caught mid-motion.

02 · Atmosphere

Color, Light &
Controlled Rupture

Color is used with restraint and intention. A dominant palette of cool grays and deep blues grounds the work in atmosphere, while selective warm tones (amber, gold) create moments of rupture and focus. This tension between cool and warm, between control and release, mirrors the conceptual core of the piece: identity as something in constant reformation.

Making space by playing with visual elements and the harmony of color to create an eerie, charged environment. The reshaped and sharp letterforms indicate dynamism and mobility, emphasizing the seriousness of transformation, not as destruction, but as becoming.

Metamorph M.O.N.A. marks the beginning of a longer inquiry into responsive, emotionally intelligent environments: spaces that do not merely exist, but react, shift, and breathe in relation to human presence.
